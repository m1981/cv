# CV Reframing: Before vs. After Comparison

## Profile Section

### Before
> Creative engineer passionate about transforming developer experiences through AI-powered automation. Proven track record of enhancing engineering velocity by integrating LLMs into workflows, building CI/CD pipelines, and creating custom developer tools. Combines hands-on Python expertise with practical AI implementation to solve real engineering challenges.

### After
> Technical leader with a strong track record of **helping large teams adopt AI-powered ways of working**. Experienced in driving technology adoption across big organisations — from leading DevOps transformation across 800+ developers to running hands-on AI workshops and prompt engineering coaching. Brings deep engineering credibility together with practical experience **helping professionals use LLMs and agentic AI tools** in their daily work. Effective at bridging the gap between what technology can do and what people are ready to do with it, with genuine awareness of the human side of change.

### What Changed
- The centre of gravity shifts from *"I build things"* to *"I help people adopt new ways of working"*
- The "800+ developers" adoption metric is moved to the front as a primary statement
- Agentic AI is explicitly mentioned (reflecting genuine proficiency)
- The human side of technology adoption is given space
- No facts were altered — only the emphasis and framing were restructured

## Final Note

Every factual claim in the reframed CV corresponds to genuine professional experience. No achievements have been invented, exaggerated, or reassigned. The transformation lies entirely in **how the existing material is organised, prioritised, and described** — not in what is claimed. The language has been kept natural and at a B2/C1 corporate level — professional enough for a senior role, but written the way a real person would write it, not a machine.
