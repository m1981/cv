# CV Analysis: Current State vs. Atos AI Adoption Role

## What the Role Requires

Based on the description provided, Atos is looking for someone to **educate and coach L1/L2 managers** in the effective use of AI tools within enterprise environments. The role therefore needs:

| What They Are Looking For | Key Signals in a Candidate |
|---|---|
| Ability to teach non-technical professionals | Training delivery, coaching, facilitation, enablement |
| Practical understanding of AI tools in business contexts | LLM fluency, agentic AI, prompt engineering, workflow integration |
| Proven experience in technology adoption | Driving adoption at scale, transformation leadership, onboarding |
| Credibility when working with enterprise-level managers | Leadership, cross-functional influence, large-scale impact |
| Awareness of the learner's concerns and resistance | Understanding change fatigue, meeting people where they are |

## Key Insight

Your CV has a **genuine and uncommon strength** for this role: real experience in **driving adoption at scale in a large enterprise** (Nokia, 800+ developers). Most people applying for AI enablement roles either have deep technical knowledge but poor people skills, or good people skills but no technical credibility. You have both — the CV just does not tell that story yet.

The Atos role is not looking for an AI researcher. It is looking for someone who can **sit down with a sceptical L2 director and help them understand how to use AI for their weekly reporting** — and you have been doing versions of this at Nokia for years.
